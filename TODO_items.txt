Actives
-------
[❌] Health Potion (1-click use, consumable)  - Restores health instantly
[❌] Mana Potion (1-click use, consumable) - Restores mana instantly
[❌] Ghost Cloak (1-click use) - Grants phasing movement and knockback immunity (like wraiths)
[❌] Gong of Weakening (1-click use) - Applies temporary weakening and armor reduction to all nearby enemies
[❌] Ice Wand (unit-targeted) - Freezes an enemy in a block of ice (like the wretched wraith ultimate)
[✔] Staff of Lightning (ground-targeted) - Summons a bolt of lightning at the target location
[❌] Updraft Tome (ground-targeted) - Applies levitation to enemies in a small radius
[❌] Tome of Duplication  (1-click use, consumable) - Refreshes the cooldown on all items and spells (except ultimates)
[❌] Shadow Shifter (monsters only, 1-click-use) - Creates a temporary night aura around the hero
[❌] Pocket Portal (piglins only, consumable, ground-targeted) - Creates a tiny portal that spreads nether terrain
[❌] War Horn (1-click-use) - Grants temporary strength to nearby friendly units

[🟡] Totem of Regeneration + (ground-targeted, consumable) - Summons an immobile attackable totem that grants regeneration to nearby friendly units
[🟡] Totem of Shielding + (ground-targeted, consumable) - Summons an immobile attackable totem that grants absorption to nearby friendly units
[🟡] Totem of Protection + (ground-targeted, consumable) - Summons an immobile attackable totem that grants resistance to nearby friendly units
[🟡] Totem of Casting + (ground-targeted, consumable) - Summons an immobile attackable totem that grants mana regen and cooldown reduction to nearby friendly units
