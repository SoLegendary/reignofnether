Balance
-------
[❌] Slimes and magma cubes now roll on attack too
[❌] Add roof to dark watchtower

[✔] Militia Bows cost reduced from (250 food, 500 wood) to (200 food, 400 wood)
[✔] Blacksmith Chainmail Armour cost reduced from 40 ore to 30 ore

[🟡] The Enchanter's Protective Enchantment has been replaced with Enchanted Tether
    - Tether to up to 1/2/3 nearby allies for 30s
    - Tethered units take half damage and gain the enchanter's damage and passive item stats
    - March of Progress raises the damage and item bonus by 50%

[❌] Villager reworks
    - Free-attacking vexes
    - Siege tank mode for ravager artillery (by burrowing them partially into the ground)
    - Royal guard ult strength from II to I but unpushable
    - Block animations with block displays for royal guard slam
        - And make slam single click like war stomp
    - Make custom arrow entity to control logic better
        - Halve pierce and multishot damage and improve other enchants

[✔] Bat HP reduced from 15hp to 10hp
[🟡] Ghasts can now benefit from increased attack damage (explosion damage is scaled accordingly)
[🟡] Piglin merchant TNT damage rescaled from 13.3/20/26.7 to 16/20/24 (radius unchanged)
[🟡] Throwable TNT items are now equivalent to a level 3 merchant TNT

[🟡] Neutral buildings now drop regular resources as bounty again instead of emeralds
