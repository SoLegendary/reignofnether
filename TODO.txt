Quality of Life
---------------
[✔] You can now use `/rtsapi-teams-mode none` on any map to disable all start blocks
[✔] Added an option to make a custom building not draw aggro from player units
    - This means idle and attack-moving units won't automatically target it
[✔] Moved the iron golem default spawn to beside the furnaces to reduce chance of getting stuck on nearby buildings
[✔] On a countdown game start, your camera is now automatically moved to your starting position
[✔] The F7 debug menu has been expanded to include navmesh visualisations
[✔] Added a random faction button to the new start menu
    - The normal menu does not have it yet (as I'm considering removing this later for full release)
