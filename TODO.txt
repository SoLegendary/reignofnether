Quality of Life
---------------
[✔] Button to toggle building/unit underlines in map menu
[✔] Grizzly and polar bears now have an attack animation
[🟡] You can now adjust spawn buttons in custom buildings by setting these nbt tags on the spawn eggs:
    - Numbers: 'foodCost', 'woodCost', 'oreCost', 'popCost', 'ticksToTrain'
    - Strings: 'tooltipTitle', 'tooltipLine1', 'tooltipLine2', etc.
    - You can add these to an existing spawn egg by dropping it on the ground and using:
       /data modify entity @e[type=item,limit=1,sort=nearest] Item.tag merge value {foodCost:100,woodCost:75,oreCost:50,popCost:2,ticksToTrain:300}
       /data modify entity @e[type=item,limit=1,sort=nearest] Item.tag merge value {tooltipTitle:"Super Zombie",tooltipLine1:"An elite zombie with diamond equipment"}
