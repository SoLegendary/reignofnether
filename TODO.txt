Balance
-------
[✅] The Enchanter's Civil Enchantment has been replaced with Protective Enchantment
[✅] Enchanter abilities no longer give experience
[✅] Enchanter March of Progress mana cost per second raised from 3 to 3.5
[✅] Pillager damage reduced from 8 to 6 (except when mounted)
[✅] Militia Archer damage reduced from 3 to 2.5
[✅] Militia Archer Training has been moved to the Town Centre and requires a Barracks
[✅] Vindicator Sharpness damage raised from +2 to +3

[✅] Headhunter damage reduced from 6 to 4.5
[✅] Brute Shields damage reduction reduced from 75% to 66%
[✅] Strider HP increased from 30 to 40

[✅] Skeleton and Stray damage reduced from 4 to 3
[✅] Bogged attack damage reduced from 2 to 1
[✅] Bogged poison damage increased from 6 to 7
[✅] Strays now have a 25% chance to create wraith snow on hit instead of slowness


Quality of Life
---------------
[✅] Aquatic mobs (drowned, scout dogs/cats, frostwalker heroes) now always travel directly over water
[✅] Added `/gamerule hideEnemyPicks` which hides enemy faction picks
[✅] Various fog of war stability improvements
[✅] Miscellaneous performance improvements, especially for large scale battles
[✅] Reduced the size of the mod jar significantly
[✅] Improved permissions security of various serverside actions
[✅] `/rtsapi-start-scenario-role` renamed to `/rtsapi player start-scenario-role` and is now compatible with player selectors
[✅] Added bees to the sandbox unit menu
[✅] Holding ctrl when issuing a move command makes your army move together in formation:
    - Faster units slow down by up to 40% to allow slower units to catch up
    - Ranged units try to stay behind melee units


Bugfixes
--------
[✅] You can no longer see your opponent's building outlines in fog at the start of a match
[✅] Joining a world with a low Y level no longer makes your camera slide down
[✅] Workers no longer get stuck trying to reach resources out of reach and instead search elsewhere
[✅] Fixed heroes sometimes spinning on spawn
[✅] Fixed various memory leaks related to closing/reopening singleplayer maps on the same session
[✅] Fixed a crash when publishing a scenario map on a server world
[✅] Fixed accuracy problems when using `/rtsapi player camera move` with different pan durations
