Slime rolling
-------------
[✔] Slimes and magma cubes now roll to move instead of jumping, meaning they can now use the new rtsPathfinding!
[✔] When close to an attack target (unit or building) they will revert back to jumping motions for attacks
[✔] Movement speed has been reduced from 27 to 25, but they now glide up stairs smoothly
[✔] You can set the 'forceJumping' flag in data to make them follow the old behaviour, eg:
    /summon reignofnether:slime_unit ~ ~ ~ {ForgeData:{forceJumping:1b}}
