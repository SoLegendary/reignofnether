[🟡] Changed the /gamerule randomItemDrops options:
    0 - disabled (except items given to units by mapmakers)
    1 - enabled, players get completely different drops
    2 - enabled, players get the same rarity of drops (NEW DEFAULT)
    3 - enabled, players get the exact same drops

[🟡] A particular rarity of item is guaranteed for each item dropped if mode 2 or 3 is set:
    - 1st drop: common
    - 3rd drop: uncommon
    - 5th drop: rare
    - 7th drop: epic
    - All other drops are: 40% common, 30% uncommon, 20% rare, 10% epic
    - There are never any repeated items (if somehow you got all 40 items in one match, no more items will drop)
