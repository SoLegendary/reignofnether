Balance
-------
[❌] Slimes and magma cubes now roll on attack too
[❌] Add roof to dark watchtower

[✅] The Enchanter's Protective Enchantment has been replaced with Enchanted Tether
    - Tether to up to 1/2/3 nearby allies for 30s
    - Tethered units take half damage and gain the enchanter's damage and passive item stats
    - March of Progress raises the damage and item bonus by 50%

[✅] Mounted Ravagers can now toggle 'Siege Mode', while active:
    - The ravager is immobile, immune to knockback, cannot rotate and can only attack enemies its facing
    - The mounted pillager gains double range and larger explosions on non-multishot arrows
    - If the pillager dies or is ejected, siege mode is disabled

[✅] Militia Bows cost reduced from (250 food, 500 wood) to (200 food, 400 wood)
[✅] Blacksmith Chainmail Armour cost reduced from 40 ore to 30 ore
[✅] Libraries can now autocast any number of enchantments instead of just one
[✅] Windcaller levitation duration increased from 4s to 5s
[✅] Library enchantments can now be stacked
[✅] Pillager base damage raised from 7 to 8
[✅] The Enchanter's Ultimate now affects multishot (increasing the arrow count to 5)
[✅] Multishot additional arrows now deal 50% less damage per level of multishot (arrows 2-3 deal 50%, arrows 4-5 deal 25%)
[✅] Arrows which have pierced a unit now deal 50% less damage (unless they are multishot arrows)
[✅] Multishot cost reduced from (70 wood, 35 ore) to (60 wood, 30 ore)
[✅] Quick charge cost reduced from (40 wood, 20 ore) to (30 wood, 20 ore)
[✅] Sharpness cost reduced from (40 wood, 60 ore) to (30 wood, 60 ore)

[✅] Great hammers now affect abilities and summoned units at 50% effectiveness (2x building damage)
[✅] Phantoms now deal 30% less damage to buildings
[✅] Wildfire Molten Bombs now deal 20 damage to buildings

[✅] Royal Guard slow from Taunting Cry reduced 60% to 40% and resistance increased from 60% to 80%
[✅] Bat HP reduced from 15hp to 10hp
[✅] Ghast explosive damage now benefits from increased attack damage
[✅] Piglin merchant TNT damage rescaled from 13.3/20/26.7 to 16/20/24 (radius unchanged)
[✅] Throwable TNT items are now equivalent to a level 3 merchant TNT
[✅] Neutral buildings now drop regular resources as bounty again instead of emeralds

[✅] All units can now take unlimited instances of damage in a single tick

[❌] Free attacking vexes?
[❌] Block animations with block displays for royal guard slam
[❌] Make mace slam single click like war stomp
